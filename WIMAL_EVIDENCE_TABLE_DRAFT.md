## Data-integrity corrections already identified

1. The handoff’s `DC Colombo 29767/MR` conflicts with the official Supreme Court document’s `DC Colombo 29769/MR`. The PDF must not choose either value until reconciled.
2. The handoff’s CIABOC amount (approximately Rs. 75 million) conflicts with CIABOC’s own 2017 notice (Rs. 50 million). The official figure is preferred for the evidence table; do not collapse this into a finding of guilt.
3. The handoff’s Court of Appeal judge/counsel list for `PHC 0044/2017` does not match the official judgment. The official judgment is the only source to use for that proceeding.
